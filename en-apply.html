Admission Channels

Which channel does AIM-I use?

Indigenous students in Taiwan have five pathways to university. AIM-I uses "Indigenous Programme Separate Enrolment" — a dedicated pathway designed exclusively for indigenous students. It has completely separate quotas from regular channels and does not conflict with them.

① Special Talent Selection

University-run, may not require GSAT, values special talents and learning portfolio.

② Star Recommendation

Based on school grades, recommended by high school. Additional indigenous quota available.

③ Individual Application

GSAT scores + portfolio review + interview. Additional indigenous quota available.

④ Exam Placement

AST scores. Indigenous students receive +10% (or +35% with language proficiency certification).

⑤ Indigenous Programme Separate Enrolment ← AIM-I's pathway

University-run enrolment designed exclusively for indigenous students. Separate from all regular quotas. Does not conflict with other channels (except if admitted through Star Recommendation — see FAQ).

Eligibility & Scholarships

What can you apply for?

Eligibility

Applicants must hold indigenous identity status (official documentation required). Current or past senior high school or vocational school graduates are welcome to apply.

Documents to Prepare

  • Indigenous identity documentation (household registration or tribal certificate)
  • High school transcript or academic record
  • Portfolio materials (personal statement, study plan, work samples — per prospectus)
  • Interview (in-person or online — per annual prospectus)

※ Whether indigenous language proficiency certification is required depends on the annual prospectus. Please contact us to confirm.

Scholarship Resources

  • Council of Indigenous Peoples scholarship
  • NKNU indigenous student grant
  • County-level indigenous affairs subsidies

Contact us — we'll help you identify every resource available to you.

FAQ

Questions you might want to ask

Do I need a business background to apply?

Not at all. This programme builds foundational knowledge in ageing and industry from the ground up. All you need is curiosity and a connection to your cultural identity.

Do I need to live in Kaohsiung?

Not necessarily. The university provides dormitory applications, and students from outside Kaohsiung can apply with priority. We also have resources to help you settle in.

What careers can I pursue after graduating?

Long-term care manager, AgeTech entrepreneur, community organisation leader, cultural entrepreneur, government indigenous affairs officer — Taiwan's super-ageing society is rapidly expanding these roles.

What scholarships are available?

Indigenous students can apply for Council of Indigenous Peoples scholarships, university indigenous student grants, and county-level subsidies. Contact us — we'll help you identify every resource available to you.

I'm not sure if I'm a good fit. What should I do?

Come and talk. You don't need a complete plan before asking questions. Write to us and share what's on your mind — we respond to every message seriously.

How is this different from a regular management degree?

We use indigenous knowledge as a core methodology, not an optional elective. Every activity connects to real industry experience. From year one, you build a verifiable portfolio — not just theory.

Is this programme only for indigenous students?

Yes. AIM-I is a programme specifically for students with indigenous identity. If you have a special circumstance, please write to us — we respond to every enquiry seriously.

Will I actually be able to find work after four years?

Taiwan became a super-aged society in 2025. AgeTech, long-term care management, and community organisations are all growing fast, with a widening talent gap. The combination of cultural knowledge, ageing expertise, and management skills is exactly what the market is looking for.

Will applying here conflict with Star Recommendation or Individual Application?

In most cases, no. AIM-I's "Indigenous Programme Separate Enrolment" has completely separate quotas from regular channels. However, if you are admitted through Star Recommendation, you cannot also apply through Individual Application that year. We recommend checking the annual prospectus or contacting us directly to confirm.

Do I need indigenous language proficiency certification?

For the regular exam placement channel, holding a language proficiency certificate increases the bonus from 10% to 35%. Whether AIM-I's separate enrolment requires this certificate depends on the annual prospectus. Please contact us — we respond to every question seriously.
