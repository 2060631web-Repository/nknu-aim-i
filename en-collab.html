Why we need you

Students need
the problem you bring in.

What can't be learned in a classroom is the texture of a real problem — how complex, how contradictory, how impossible to solve with a standard answer.

The problem you bring in — even one that feels ordinary to you — may be the best design starting point for a student.

And you will meet a new generation genuinely curious about your work — bringing cultural perspectives and service design tools, offering you possibilities you hadn't considered.

"Your real problems are the best curriculum. Whatever the size of your organisation, there is a place for you to contribute."

— AIM-I Collaboration Philosophy
🤝 Collaboration photo

Three ways

Choose the way
that fits you best.

01
🏢

Silver Industry Field Visit

Open your space and let students in. Twice per semester — you provide the venue, students bring back real questions.

02
🎯

Ageing Industry Sprint

Name the problem you're facing. Students take it as their brief — completing service design and a business proposal, then presenting to you at Demo Day.

03
🤝

Career Navigation Mentor

Four times a year. One hour per session in a small group. You share what you've learned in the field — students bring proposals they've just finished.
